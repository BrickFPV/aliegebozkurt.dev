What I Do

Four core engineering areas from bench to deployment.

Embedded & Custom PCBs

Circuit design, power regulation, and SMD board assembly.

  • Digital Variable PSU: Programmable 1V–17.5V digital bench supply with live voltage & current telemetry.
  • SMD Rework: Fine-pitch soldering, hot-air assembly & sensor integration.
KiCad PCB Design ESP32 / ESP8266 Power Electronics SMD Rework
02

FPV Drones & 3D Fabrication

Custom 1S micro-quads and Klipper-tuned hardware prototyping.

  • Custom 1S Tinywhoops: Hand-built 75mm indoor/freestyle micro-drones with ELRS.
  • Klipper 3D Printing: Raspberry Pi-driven Ender-3 printing structural mounts & ducts.
  • Mechatronics: Pan-tilt servo mechanisms & 3D-printable Delta kinematics.

Languages, Vision & Applied AI

Systems programming, image processing libraries, and edge AI.

  • TARS AI Robot: Dual-axis OpenCV face tracking & voice interaction on gpt-oss 120B.
  • Edge Vehicle Detection: 160° wide-angle Raspberry Pi pipeline using YOLO11 Nano NCNN.
Cicekli Script wit (CV) C / Python OpenCV YOLO11 NCNN

Linux Infra & LoRa Mesh RF

Self-hosted Docker services and off-grid 868 MHz telemetry.

  • Debian Home Server: CasaOS, Docker, FastAPI microservices, WireGuard VPN & Pi-hole.
